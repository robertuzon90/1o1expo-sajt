About Ambiente Frankfurt

Ambiente is Messe Frankfurt's international trade fair for consumer goods: tableware, kitchen and household, interiors, gifts and products for the workplace. It runs alongside Christmasworld, the trade fair for seasonal decorations.

In 2026, Ambiente, Christmasworld and Creativeworld together brought 4,636 exhibitors and participants from 170 countries and around 140,000 visitors to Frankfurt.

Source: Messe Frankfurt final report 2026

What works on a Ambiente stand

A few things we plan for on almost every stand at this fair:

  • Let visitors touch the products. Buyers at Ambiente want to pick things up, so we plan open shelves and tables at a comfortable height, not only glass cases.
  • Light for products, not just for the stand. Glass, ceramics and textiles look very different under good spotlights.
  • A table for orders. A quieter corner where buyers can sit down, go through the catalogue and place an order.
  • Storage for stock and packaging. Spare samples, boxes and order forms need a place out of sight.

Common questions

When should we contact you for Ambiente 2027?+

Now. For a custom stand, about five months ahead is comfortable, and that point has already passed or is close. Tell us your date in your brief and we tell you honestly whether we can still build it well.

Does your price include the floor space?+

No. You book and pay the space, electricity, water and other venue services directly with the organiser. Our all-inclusive price covers the stand itself: design, production, graphics, transport, build-up and dismantling.

Who handles the organiser's technical forms?+

You book the space and the organiser's services. We help you with the technical forms for the stand itself, such as the stand drawings the organiser asks for.

Is someone from your team at the fair?+

Yes. Our team is on site throughout the fair, so if anything on the stand needs attention, it is handled quickly.
