OUR STORY

How 1o1 expo started.

At 8:59 on opening day, the hall goes quiet. The brochures are stacked, the screens are running, the team is in position. One minute later the doors open, and all the months of work come down to one thing: the stand.

Our founder learned this at an exhibition stand company in Switzerland, where a late stand is simply not an option. He saw every stage of the work, from the first call and the offer to the design, the build-up and that last quiet minute before the doors open. 1o1 expo exists to give every client that minute: a finished stand, and nothing left to chase.
