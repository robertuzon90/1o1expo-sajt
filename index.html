HOW IT WORKS

From first message to opening day.

You always know what happens next, and nothing is produced before you say yes.

  1. STEP 1 Tell us about your fair Your fair, hall, stand size and what you want visitors to remember. Rough notes and a phone photo of the floor plan are fine.
  2. STEP 2 · ≤ 7 WORKING DAYS Get your offer A first design and one fixed price for the whole stand, so you see what you pay for.
  3. STEP 3 Approve the design We adjust the design with you until it fits. Production starts only after your yes.
  4. STEP 4 We produce, deliver and build Walls, flooring, lighting and graphics are made, checked and shipped to the venue. We help you with the organiser's technical forms.
  5. OPENING DAY Walk onto a finished stand You arrive at a stand ready for visitors. When the fair closes, we take it down. You just leave.
