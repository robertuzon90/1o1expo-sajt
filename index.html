QUESTIONS

Questions we hear first.

BEFORE YOU BOOK
How much does a stand cost?+

There is no honest price per m². Size, design, materials, the venue and your dates all change it. So tell us your budget, and we design the best stand for it. Within 7 working days you get one fixed price. No obligation.

Get your quote →
Do we pay anything before we approve the design?+

No. Your design and your fixed price are free and come with no obligation. You only commit once you have seen both and said yes.

Can we talk before we decide?+

Yes. We meet online at a time that suits you and go through your fair, your space and your ideas. No sales pitch, just your stand.

What if we have no floor plan yet?+

Send what you have: the fair, the date and roughly how much space you booked. Missing details are fine, we sort them out together. Not sure which stand type you booked? Send the booking confirmation and we check.

DATES AND BUILD-UP
What if our fair is only a few weeks away?+

Tell us the date in your brief. We tell you within one working day whether we can still build it well, and if not, we say so.

How does my stand get to the fair on time?+

We plan production and delivery around the official build-up days of your fair. Your schedule shows every date before you sign, so you never have to chase a haulier.

Who is on site during build-up?+

Our build crew, working from the drawings you approved, and your project manager, who is reachable the whole time and knows your stand inside out.

What happens to the stand after the fair?+

Dismantling is part of your price. When the fair closes, our crew takes the stand down and clears your space, so you just leave.
