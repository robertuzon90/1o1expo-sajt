STAND TYPES

Which stand type is yours?

The organiser sets the number of open sides when you book your space. It decides how visitors reach you and what your stand can be. Not sure which one you have? Send us your booking and we'll check.

Row stand Walls on three sides, with room for big graphics, a storage room and a clear brand wall.
  • Open sides: 1
  • Best for: Graphics & storage
  • Space rent: Base rate
Row stands in detail → Corner stand Open on two sides, so two streams of visitors see your brand as they walk both aisles.
  • Open sides: 2
  • Best for: Two-way visibility
  • Space rent: Often higher
Corner stands in detail → Peninsula stand Open on three sides, with one strong back wall for your brand and a storage room.
  • Open sides: 3
  • Best for: A strong brand wall
  • Space rent: Higher again
Peninsula stands in detail → Island stand Open on all four sides and seen from every aisle. The biggest presence in the hall.
  • Open sides: 4
  • Best for: Maximum visibility
  • Space rent: Usually the highest

QUESTIONS

Questions we hear first.

BEFORE YOU BOOK
How much does a stand cost?

There is no honest price per m². Size, design, materials, the venue and your dates all change it. So tell us your budget, and we design the best stand for it. Within 7 working days you get one all-inclusive price. No obligation.

Do we pay anything before we approve the design?

No. Your design and your price are free and come with no obligation. You only commit once you have seen both and said yes.

Can we talk before we decide?

Yes. We meet online at a time that suits you and go through your fair, your space and your ideas. No sales pitch, just your stand.

Pick a time for a 30-min call →
What if we have no floor plan yet?

Send what you have: the fair, the date and roughly how much space you booked. Missing details are fine, we sort them out together. Not sure which stand type you booked? Send the booking confirmation and we'll check.

We already have a stand design. Can you build it?

Yes. Send us your drawings, renders or the design from your last fair, and we come back with one all-inclusive price to build it: production, transport, build-up and dismantling. If something can be built more simply or at a lower cost, we suggest it.

DATES AND BUILD-UP
What if our fair is only a few weeks away?

Tell us the date in your brief. Within one working day, we tell you honestly whether we can still build it well.

How does my stand get to the fair on time?

We plan production and delivery around the official build-up days of your fair. Your schedule shows every date before you sign, so you never have to chase a haulier.

Who is on site during build‑up?

Our build crew, working from the drawings you approved. Our team is reachable the whole time and knows your stand inside out.

What happens to the stand after the fair?

Dismantling is part of your price. When the fair closes, our crew takes the stand down and clears your space, so you just leave.

Six quick fields, two minutes. The floor plan is optional and missing details are fine. We sort them out together.
