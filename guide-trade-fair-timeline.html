Your trade fair timeline: what to do and when

Exhibiting runs on deadlines. Here is what to do nine months, six months, three months, six weeks and one week before the fair opens, and what happens after it closes.

By the 1o1 expo team · Updated October 2026

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Apply for your space early. Placement proposals often go out six to nine months before the fair.
  • For a custom stand, brief your stand builder about four to six months ahead.
  • Most organiser deadlines fall four to ten weeks before build-up: drawings, catalogue entry and services.
  • Late orders often cost more, so put the organiser's dates in your calendar as soon as you get them.
Every fair sets its own dates. Use this timeline as a guide, and check the deadlines in your organiser's exhibitor area.
1
9–12 MONTHS BEFORE

Book your space

  • Apply for your stand space. Organisers usually send placement proposals months ahead, and the best spaces go first.
  • Confirm the proposal quickly. Some fairs give you only about a week to accept.
  • Set your goals and a first budget range for the whole stand.
  • Read the participation conditions and the price list, including fees on top of the space rent.
2
ABOUT 6 MONTHS BEFORE

Brief your stand builder

  • Send your brief: fair, stand size, open sides, goals and budget range.
  • Compare designs and prices, and ask what each quote includes.
  • Book hotels. Rooms near the venue sell out early for big fairs, and prices rise as the date gets closer.
  • Decide who will work on the stand and on which days.

For a custom stand, this is the comfortable moment to start. It leaves time for changes and for the organiser's approval deadlines.

3
3–4 MONTHS BEFORE

Approve the design

  • Approve the final design and price, so production can be planned.
  • If your stand needs approval, for example a double-deck, a very tall or a large stand, prepare the drawings now. Submission often opens months ahead, and the deadline is usually six to eight weeks before build‑up.
  • Prepare the content for graphics: messages, product photos and logos.
  • Plan which products and samples travel to the fair.
4
6–8 WEEKS BEFORE

Organiser deadlines

  • Enter your company in the fair catalogue and online directory before the media deadline.
  • Order electricity, water, internet and hanging points before the early deadlines.
  • Send final graphics files for print.
  • Order exhibitor passes and invite your customers to visit the stand.

Many fairs add a surcharge to services ordered after the deadline.

5
1–4 WEEKS BEFORE

Final checks

  • Apply for extra build-up or dismantling time if you need it. At some fairs this is due about a month before.
  • Book truck slots or parking if your own deliveries come to the hall.
  • Brief the stand team: goals, who to talk to, how to capture leads.
  • Check the final schedule for build-up, opening and dismantling.
6
BUILD-UP AND OPENING DAY

Walk onto a finished stand

  • Build-up runs on the days the organiser sets, usually two days to a week before opening.
  • Check the finished stand before the doors open: graphics, lighting, power and storage.
  • Keep the stand tidy and stocked during the fair.
7
AFTER THE FAIR

Dismantle and follow up

  • The stand comes down on the official dismantling days.
  • Follow up with every lead while the fair is still fresh in their minds.
  • Expect the organiser's final invoice for services a few weeks later.
  • Write down what worked and what to change for next year.

Common questions

How early should we contact a stand builder?+

For a custom stand, four to six months before the fair is comfortable. It leaves time to compare designs, make changes and meet the organiser's drawing deadlines.

Our fair is only a few weeks away. Is it too late?+

Tell us the date in your brief. We tell you within one working day whether we can still build it well, and if not, we say so.

Who handles the organiser's forms?+

You book the space and the organiser's services, such as electricity and passes. We help you with the technical forms for the stand itself.

Do all fairs have the same deadlines?+

No. Each organiser publishes its own dates, usually as a checklist or an order deadlines page in the exhibitor area. Add them to your calendar as soon as you get access.
